This example shows how to generate AI embedding vectors on your 4D entities and use them for semantic search.

What are embeddings?
An embedding is a vector of numbers that represents the meaning of a text: texts with similar meaning produce close vectors.
For example, searching for "apps that are easy to use" finds Grace, whose biography talks about intuitive interfaces and usability tests, although no word matches.

Who does what?
• 4D natively stores vectors (4D.Vector class) and runs vector similarity queries with query().
• 4D AIKit calls the AI provider through the model aliases defined in 4D Settings > AI.
• 4D Embedding Studio does the rest: it builds the text sent to the model from your fields, computes vectors in batch, and flags them as dirty when the data changes.

What does this HDI demonstrate?
Each step has its own tab:
1. Configure AI settings – configure your AI provider and the model alias in 4D Settings > AI
2. Configure Embeddings – define the vector field, its phrase template and its model alias in the Embedding Studio
3. Generate Embeddings – compute the vectors of the Students records, all of them or only the missing and dirty ones
4. Update Records – edit a student and see its vector flagged as dirty, ready to be recomputed
5. Search Embeddings – find students semantically with a plain-text query

What do I need?
4D 21 R5 or later and an AI provider supporting embedding models.
This project is preconfigured for OpenAI with the text-embedding-3-large model: you only need to enter your API key on the Configure AI settings tab.
You can also use another provider, such as a local Ollama server with an embedding model (e.g. embeddinggemma).

No vector is shipped with this demo: compute them on the Generate Embeddings tab before searching.
